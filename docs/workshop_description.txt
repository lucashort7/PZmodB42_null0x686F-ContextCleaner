[h1]null0x686F ContextCleaner[/h1]

A context menu cleaner for Project Zomboid Build 42. Declutter right-click menus in real time, so you stop scrolling past mods you don't care about to find the option you actually want.

Originally based on [b]Context Cleaner[/b] by Anach, full credit to the original concept. This version is a complete rewrite, built after the original started costing noticeable time on menus under a heavy modlist.

[h2]🎯 Key Features[/h2]

[list]
[*] ⚡ [b]Nothing runs until you open a menu[/b]. No [i]OnTick[/i], no [i]OnPlayerUpdate[/i], nothing per frame. The mod is idle until a context menu is built.
[*] 🥇 [b]It sees what other mods added[/b]. The handler removes and re-registers itself on every fire, which puts it last in the queue, so it filters the finished menu instead of a half-built one.
[*] 🖱️ [b]Works on both menus[/b]. Filters [i]OnFillInventoryObjectContextMenu[/i] and [i]OnFillWorldObjectContextMenu[/i] independently.
[*] 🪟 [b]Dedicated config UI[/b]. The [i]ContextCleanerWindow[/i], no digging through text files to add a rule.
[*] ✂️ [b]Hide or Fold[/b]. Remove clutter outright, or tuck it into a collapsible submenu instead of losing it completely.
[*] 🔍 [b]Flexible matching[/b]. Exact text, wildcard (*), or full Lua patterns for power users.
[*] 💾 [b]Persistent config[/b]. Rules saved to [i]Zomboid/Lua/ContextCleaner_preset_default.txt[/i], survives between sessions.
[/list]

[h2]⚙️ How To Use[/h2]

[list]
[*] Press [b]Numpad 7[/b] (default keybind) to open the ContextCleaner window. That is the only way in, there's no menu entry for it (on purpose, it keeps the menus you're trying to clean uncluttered too 😄).
[*] Add a rule with 4 fields:
[/list]

[list]
[*] [b]Pattern[/b]. The text to match against a menu option's name.
[*] [b]Type[/b]. [i]exact[/i] (literal match), [i]wildcard[/i] (use [b]*[/b] for anything), or [i]luapattern[/i] (raw [url=https://www.lua.org/pil/20.2.html]Lua pattern[/url] for advanced matching).
[*] [b]Action[/b]. [i]hide[/i] (remove it) or [i]fold[/i] (collapse it into a submenu instead).
[*] [b]Scope[/b]. [i]all[/i], [i]inventory[/i], or [i]world[/i], which menu the rule applies to.
[/list]

[h2]🧠 A Word on Performance[/h2]

Here is the honest shape of it, because a mod that sells itself on performance owes you the real number. When a menu opens, ContextCleaner walks the options once and checks each one against your rules. That means the work is [i]options × rules[/i], and the rule count is the half you control.

Stacking 30+ [i]exact[/i]/[i]wildcard[/i] rules means 30+ comparisons per menu option. In practice you will never feel it, but the cost is real and it grows with your list. Prefer a handful of well-written [b]Lua Patterns[/b] over a long list of exact matches. One good pattern can replace a dozen individual rules, and it is faster for the same result.
